About

Close, quiet, and there early

I photograph the rooms and streets where a community decides things about itself: Friday-night sidelines, summer league courts, marches down Bardstown Road. The approach is documentary — no staging, minimal direction, available light wherever it can carry the frame.

Assignment and editorial work for local press, teams, and organizers. Private sessions for families and athletes on the same terms: I stay out of the way and hand back the day as it looked.
